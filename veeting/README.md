# Veeting

**Voice-first meetings, appointments, and notes — for people who get work done from their phone.**

Veeting is a minimal mobile app with one job: make it effortless to book time slots, arrange meetings, and capture notes — by voice or text — while you're on the move.

## Core Idea

Connect to your **calendar** and **email**, then get out of the way:

- 📅 **Book time slots** — see availability, propose times, confirm meetings
- 🎙️ **Voice commands** — "book a meeting with Sam tomorrow at 3"
- 📝 **Notes** — capture voice or text notes, attached to meetings or standalone
- 📧 **Email integration** — turn email threads into scheduled meetings

## Principles

1. **So simple, so minimal** — no feature creep, every screen earns its place
2. **Focused product** — scheduling + notes, nothing else
3. **Phone-first** — designed for one hand, on the go
4. **Voice as a first-class input** — not a bolted-on gimmick
